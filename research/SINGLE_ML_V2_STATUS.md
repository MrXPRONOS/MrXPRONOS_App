A 35%-DC fixed comparator is retained. Every frozen pregame ML/DC prediction is retained in memory only after the whole UTC match-day predictions; its settled actual score can calibrate weights solely from earlier dates. Weight candidates 0..1 in 0.05 increments are selected by recency-weighted log loss, subject to a minimum 80 prior forecasts over 12 days; otherwise a declared fallback is used. Calibration refits on a 30-day cycle; the history of selected weights and paired same-match comparisons are in `summary.json`. Models output `machine_learning_hybrid` (calibrated) and `machine_learning_hybrid_fixed` (fixed). Dedicated tests added.
